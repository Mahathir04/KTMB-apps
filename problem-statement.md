# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
KTMB application users, particularly first-time users, daily commuters, and passengers who frequently book train tickets.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Users may experience difficulties navigating the application due to unclear menus, complicated booking procedures, and poor organization of important information such as train schedules and ticket details.

## Why does it matter?
These usability issues can increase the time and effort required to complete bookings, cause user frustration, and reduce overall user satisfaction. They may also discourage users from using the application for future travel arrangements.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
The KTMB application is used by passengers to search for train schedules and purchase tickets. However, users, particularly first-time users, may experience difficulties navigating the application due to unclear menus, complicated booking procedures, and poorly organized information. These usability issues can increase the time and effort required to complete bookings, resulting in user frustration and a less satisfying experience. Therefore, improvements to the application's user interface and navigation are needed to provide a more efficient, accessible, and user-friendly booking experience.
